FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Everything you need to know before booking your first shipment with us.

How quickly can a vehicle be arranged?

For most city and metro routes, we can confirm a vehicle within 1–2 hours. For interstate and ODC moves, we typically confirm within the same day, depending on route and cargo type.

Do you handle ODC and over-dimensional cargo?

Yes. We handle over-dimensional cargo with route surveys, permits coordination and pilot escort vehicles. Share the dimensions and weight and we'll plan the entire move for you.

What documents are required for booking?

Just basic details — sender name, contact number, pickup and drop location, cargo description and preferred date. For container or ODC moves, additional documents may be needed for permits.

Is the pricing fixed, or does it vary?

We quote a fixed price based on route, vehicle type, weight and any special handling requirements. There are no surprise charges added at delivery. Tolls and state permits are usually included unless stated otherwise.

Do you provide warehousing support?

Yes — we offer short and long-term storage with cross-docking options, which is useful when shipments need to pause between transit legs or wait for customs clearance.

Which cities do you serve?

We cover all major metros and industrial hubs across India — Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, and many more. If your destination isn't listed, contact us and we'll confirm availability.

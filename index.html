About Us

A decade of dependable freight movement.

RM Transport Co. is a Mumbai-based logistics company serving businesses across India with FTL, ODC and project cargo services.

From our hub in Vasai East, we dispatch a modern fleet of Tempo 407, LPT trucks, ODC trailers and 40 FT trailers to every corner of the country. Our operational discipline, route planning and real-time coordination ensure your cargo arrives safely — every single time.

We work with manufacturers, distributors, EPC contractors and 3PL partners who rely on us for consistent, on-time delivery at fair rates.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Still have questions? Reach out via WhatsApp — we reply in minutes.

How quickly can you pick up my cargo? +

For urgent shipments within Mumbai and surrounding areas, we can arrange pickup within 2–4 hours. For pan-India transport, we typically schedule pickup within 24 hours of confirmation.

Do you provide transit insurance? +

Yes. All our shipments are covered under marine transit insurance. We coordinate with your existing policy or arrange coverage on request.

Which states do you cover? +

We operate across all 28 states in India with valid pan-India permits. Our regular routes include Mumbai–Delhi, Mumbai–Chennai, Mumbai–Kolkata, Mumbai–Bangalore, and Mumbai–Hyderabad.

How is pricing calculated? +

Pricing depends on distance, load weight/volume, vehicle type, and route. We provide transparent, all-inclusive quotes — no hidden charges. Share your details for a quote in 30 minutes.

Can you handle ODC and project cargo? +

Absolutely. We have specialised ODC trailers (20 FT, 24 FT, 32 FT) and 40 FT trailers. Our team handles route surveys, permits, and escort coordination for oversized loads.

Do you offer real-time tracking? +

Yes. All our vehicles are GPS-tracked. You'll receive regular updates via WhatsApp or can call our dispatch desk 24/7 for live location and ETA.
